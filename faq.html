Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for orders, recommendations, shipping, returns, and account support.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 6 business days. Express delivery is available at checkout.

Can I return a book?

Yes. You can return unread books in original condition within 14 days of delivery.

Do you offer recommendations?

Yes. Use the contact page or browse sections by mood and genre from the shop page.

Can I track my order?

Tracking details are emailed once the order is packed and handed to the carrier.

Are digital books available?

This demo catalog focuses on physical books, but the layout is ready for digital formats too.
